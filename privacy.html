2. Information We Collect

2.1 Information You Provide

  • Account information: Email address and password (passwords are hashed and never stored in plaintext) when you register. If you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive a unique identifier and the email address associated with that account.
  • Apple “Hide My Email”: When you use Sign in with Apple, you may choose Apple’s “Hide My Email” feature. In this case, Apple provides us with a private relay address (in the form of xxx@privaterelay.appleid.com) instead of your real email address. We use this relay address as your contact email for all service communications. Apple forwards messages we send to your real address. You can revoke this relay at any time through your Apple ID settings.
  • Profile data: Information you share during onboarding, including goals, work context, and preferences.
  • Content: Tasks, messages, notes, and other content you submit through the Service.
  • Communications: Information you send when contacting support.

2.2 Information Collected Automatically

  • Usage data: How you interact with the App (features used, screens viewed, session length).
  • Device data: Device type, OS version, app version, language, time zone, and unique device identifiers (used for push notifications).
  • Diagnostic data: Crash logs and performance metrics.

2.3 Information We Do Not Collect

  • We do not collect precise location data.
  • We do not access your contacts, photos, calendar, or microphone except when you explicitly grant permission for a specific feature.
  • We do not currently use any third-party advertising SDKs, marketing trackers, or analytics SDKs in the App.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the Service
  • Personalize task suggestions and AI responses
  • Send service-related notifications (e.g., reminders, account confirmations)
  • Send optional marketing communications, where you have consented
  • Process subscriptions and manage your account
  • Respond to support requests
  • Monitor and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents
  • Comply with legal obligations

4. Legal Basis for Processing (GDPR / UK GDPR)

If you are located in the EEA or UK, we process your personal data on the following legal bases:

  • Legitimate interests — To improve the Service, prevent abuse, and ensure security (Article 6(1)(f))
  • Consent — Where required, including for optional marketing communications (Article 6(1)(a))
  • Legal obligation — Where we are required by law to process data (Article 6(1)(c))

5. AI Processing

TopTaskAI uses artificial intelligence to help you manage tasks and conversations. To do this, the content you submit (messages, tasks, goals) is sent to Anthropic, PBC for processing by Claude, their large language model.

Per Anthropic’s commercial terms, inputs and outputs sent through their API are not used to train their models. Anthropic processes this data only to return responses to the Service and may retain it briefly for safety and abuse monitoring before deletion.

For more details, see Anthropic’s privacy policy at https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy.

12. Account Deletion

You can delete your account at any time from within the App: Settings → Delete Account. This permanently and irreversibly deletes your account, all your content (messages, tasks, profile data, streaks, and check-in history), and all associated personal data from our active systems immediately. Residual copies in encrypted database backups are purged within 30 days.

Important: Deleting your TopTaskAI account does not automatically cancel your App Store or Play Store subscription. To stop subscription renewals, you must separately cancel your subscription through your Apple ID Subscriptions settings (iOS) or Google Play Store subscription settings (Android). TopTaskAI cannot cancel platform subscriptions on your behalf.

If you cannot access the App, email privacy@toptaskai.com and we will process the deletion manually within 30 days.
